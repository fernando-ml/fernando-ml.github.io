## Research question

Can domain-specific models distinguish human-authored graduate application materials from AI-generated or AI-revised versions?

## My contribution

As a coauthor, I contributed to software, data curation, validation, and analysis. The study investigates letters of recommendation and statements of intent, with a public codebase.

## Evaluation context

The findings concern the domains, datasets, and models evaluated in the study. They do not establish a universal AI-text detector. The paper describes the experimental setup and limitations.
