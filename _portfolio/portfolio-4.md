## Question

How can two groups of participants be matched according to their preferences while preventing pairs that would both prefer to leave their assigned matches?

## Scope

Studied the stable marriage (stable matching) problem, its game-theoretic structure, and applications. The report and slides explain the problem and solution concepts.
