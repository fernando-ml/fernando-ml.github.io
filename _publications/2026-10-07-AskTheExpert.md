Ask the Expert summarizes challenging cyber-defense states, requests constrained recommendations from an LLM, and converts that advice into reward bonuses for PPO. Experiments on CAGE Challenge 1 and 2 compare learning efficiency against PPO and potential-based reward-shaping baselines.

Accepted at IEEE GLOBECOM 2026; proceedings forthcoming. The linked version is the public preprint.
